# MFOX v1.4.1 — Companies Discovery

## Goal
Restore Companies as a first-class top-level discovery surface that shows the breadth of possible menstrual-fluid utilization, without weakening the evidence-linked knowledge graph introduced in v1.4.0.

## Two complementary company views

### Companies
A broad translational/utilization landscape. All curated companies are discoverable whether or not they currently have a verified paper/project relationship.

Filters:
- utilization area
- material/component exploited
- development stage
- evidence status
- free-text company/technology/application search

Views:
- Utilization map
- Material → utilization table
- Company directory
- Evidence & maturity table

### Explore → Labs & companies
An evidence-connected view. When Explore filters are active, companies appear only through explicit Study ↔ Project ↔ Company links. With no Explore filters, the full curated company set remains visible.

## Data model
Four normalized discovery columns were added to `data/companies.csv`:
- `utilization_domain`
- `material_exploited`
- `maturity_level`
- `evidence_status`

The original `development_stage`, `evidence_note`, `source_url`, and `last_verified` fields are retained.

## Curation
Fox Den company entry now requires at least one utilization area and one material/component class, and records standardized maturity and evidence-status metadata. These descriptors do not create scientific evidence links.

## Interpretation
Maturity and evidence labels are descriptive categories, not rankings or endorsements. Emerging uses are intentionally retained in the discovery landscape so MFOX can represent the possible translational space of menstrual fluid.
