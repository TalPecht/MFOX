# MFOX Task 1 evidence search — v0.4

Freeze date: 2026-09-26.

## Scope
Human menstrual fluid/blood/effluent and directly derived fractions, plus menstrual-fluid-derived cultured cells retained as a separately filterable provenance class. Discovery-scale molecular profiling includes transcriptomics (bulk/single-cell/single-nucleus), proteomics, metabolomics/lipidomics, epigenomics/methylome, genomics, microbiome/metatranscriptomics, and extracellular-vesicle omics. Targeted single-marker/panel assays are excluded from the core omics landscape. Endometrial biopsy studies are excluded unless menstrual material itself is profiled.

## Search families
Menstrual synonyms: menstrual fluid; menstrual blood; menstrual effluent/effluence; menstrual-derived; menstrual blood-derived. These were crossed with: omics/multi-omics; RNA-seq/transcriptome; single-cell/scRNA-seq/single-nucleus/snRNA-seq; proteome/proteomics/mass spectrometry; metabolome/metabolomics/LC-MS/NMR; lipidome/lipidomics; methylome/DNA methylation/epigenome; microbiome/16S/ITS/metatranscriptome; extracellular vesicle/sEV; genomics/exome.

Sources checked: PubMed/PMC, GEO/BioProject where identified, journal/preprint pages, and targeted web searches for hard-to-index terminology. Endometriosis systematic reviews were used as back-checks, not as substitutes for primary-study verification.

## Important limitation
This is a systematic targeted web/PubMed audit, not yet a PRISMA-grade database search across Embase/Web of Science/Scopus. Therefore MFOX should state “curated landscape, searched through 26 Sep 2026” rather than “all studies ever published.” `search_audit.csv` records inclusions, candidates, and representative exclusions.
