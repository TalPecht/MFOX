# MFOX v0.8 — Community + cellular profiling ontology correction

Freeze date: 2026-09-26.

## Scientific corrections

1. **Community is publication-linked, not omics-linked.** Full authorship records are stored; visible Community eligibility remains first author OR last author OR corresponding author OR explicitly equal-contribution author OR >1 captured menstrual-fluid publication.
2. **Renate G. van der Molen / Radboud menstrual-blood immunology lineage added.** v0.8 adds verified menstrual-blood publications from 2014, 2018, 2022, 2024 and 2025, plus the 2023 menstrual-blood reproductive-immunology perspective. Corresponding/equal-contribution flags are recorded only when supported by article metadata/author notes.
3. **Flow cytometry is an Evidence modality.** Cellular profiling is no longer hidden under a generic label. Six additional flow-cytometry studies were added to the evidence layer; the pre-existing Warren assay was normalized to `Flow cytometry`.
4. **Microbiome ontology cleaned.** `Microbiome` is treated as a research domain, not an assay modality. The assay modality is now the measurement: `16S rRNA sequencing` or `Metatranscriptomics` where appropriate.
5. **Assay modality and biological domain separated.** `assays.csv` now contains `assay_family` and `research_domain` alongside the legacy/internal `omics_modality` field. The app labels this field “Assay modality”.

## Scope wording
MFOX maps the **omics, molecular and cellular profiling landscape** of human menstrual fluid and its derivatives. The Community bibliography is broader than the Evidence layer.

## Important completeness note
v0.8 materially expands the menstrual-fluid immune/cellular literature but is **not yet an exhaustive bibliography of every menstrual-fluid publication**. Community completeness should continue to be audited systematically rather than by adding names manually.
