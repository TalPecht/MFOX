# MFOX v1.4.0 — Connected Knowledgebase

## Design goal
MFOX is a living menstrual-fluid **knowledgebase**, not a replacement for GEO, SRA, PRIDE, institutional repositories or company data platforms. v1.4.0 makes the connections between evidence and those external resources visible.

## New entity model
The canonical evidence chain remains Study → Cohort → Biospecimen → Assay → Dataset. v1.4.0 adds Project / Program as a first-class entity with explicit many-to-many links to Studies and Companies.

## Explore
Explore is now the central reader-facing discovery surface with five views: Evidence, Papers, Datasets, Projects & programs, and Labs & companies. The summary strip shows how many entities are connected by the current filters.

## Curation
Fox Den direct entry supports Projects / programs. Curators can attach verified Study and Company links at creation time. Links are never inferred from shared names, investigators or topic similarity. Accepted project/program community submissions remain visible with a pending status until structured curation is complete.

## Seed project records
- MultiMENDo — ERC-funded menstrual-fluid multi-omics project coordinated by Université Paris Cité. No study link is asserted in the seed table without explicit relationship verification.
- NextGen Jane Menstrualome Research Program — industry research program. The existing MFOX NextGen Jane publication/company relationships are linked explicitly.
