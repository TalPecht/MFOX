## Community intake

Join the Community now stores structured academic/company metadata aligned with the public Community and Companies schemas. Profiles are private until curator review.

## Registry coverage

MFOX separates curated registry records from registry search coverage. WHO ICTRP is used as a broad discovery layer while the original registry remains canonical. Active search coverage includes ClinicalTrials.gov, ISRCTN, DRKS, the Netherlands registry, ChiCTR, ReBEC, CTIS, ANZCTR, CTRI, CRiS, JPRN/UMIN, PACTR, IRCT, TCTR and SLCTR.

A registry record is included only when menstrual fluid/blood/effluent or a menstrual-blood-derived product is actually collected, analyzed, or used; trials that only report menstrual symptoms or blood-loss scores are excluded from the registry evidence layer.
