# MFOX v0.8.1 — Maps & Networks

## Changes
- Geography is now institution-centred rather than person-centred.
- Geographic popups restore scientific context: researchers, technologies, sample types, disease/questions and research domains.
- Technology, sample and disease filters are available directly above the map.
- The map reports how many researchers have verified coordinates and how many remain unmapped, rather than silently dropping missing affiliations.
- Map bounds automatically fit the displayed institutions.
- New Scientific Network view supports:
  - researcher co-authorship;
  - technology ↔ disease/question;
  - sample type ↔ technology;
  - technology ↔ research domain.
- Network filters persist conceptually across technology/disease exploration.

## Important geography limitation
v0.8.1 does not fabricate locations. Researchers lacking a verified institution/coordinate remain explicitly counted as awaiting affiliation curation. Current and publication-time affiliation should be separated in a future affiliation-history table before historical geographic claims are made.
