# MFOX v1.1 — Workspace & usability release

## Changes
- Compact, horizontal Home screen with the MFOX logo, sample overview, technology icons, field snapshot and working navigation actions.
- Reader-facing Community inclusion explanation.
- Fixed Collaboration groups rendering (`tags$details` / `tags$summary`).
- Join the MFOX Community panel for researchers, clinicians, trainees and labs, including people who have not yet published MF work. Public submissions are review-gated and do not write directly into curated people data.
- Landscape rebuilt as a flexible workspace: filters, visualization and evidence-details panels can be collapsed, expanded, removed and restored; Reset layout restores the default.
- Landscape adds transpose and category-order controls, responsive Plotly configuration and larger margins for long labels.
- Plan-a-Study and sample terminology retain reader-facing definitions; Community and Home navigation are connected.

## Community inclusion
The publication-derived directory includes first, last or corresponding authors; explicitly equal-contributing authors; and researchers appearing on >1 eligible menstrual-fluid publication. Community-submitted researchers are a separate reviewed provenance route.
