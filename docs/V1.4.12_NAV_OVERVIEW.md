# MFOX v1.4.12 — navigation and overview consolidation

## Information architecture

### Landscape
High-level pattern view: evidence concentration, gaps, composition and change over time.

### Explore
Record-level connected evidence. Scientific relationship visualizations now live here rather than in Community.

Explore subviews:
- Studies & assays
- Connections
- Papers
- Datasets
- Projects & programs
- Labs & companies

The Connections view contains:
1. Evidence flow: sample → technology → curated studies → papers/datasets/projects/institutions/companies.
2. Scientific relationships: technology ↔ disease/question, sample ↔ technology, or technology ↔ research domain.

Both connection views follow the main Explore filters.

### Companies
Translation/utilization discovery. Evidence status and development maturity are now shown in Company directory instead of a separate Evidence & maturity tab.

Company subviews:
- Company directory
- Utilization map

### Community
People and organization discovery only:
- Researchers & labs
- Companies & innovators
- Collaboration groups
- Geography
- Join the Community

Scientific relationship networks were removed from Community because they describe the evidence base rather than the community itself.

## Entry overview pattern
Major working areas now use a compact three-part strip:
- **Purpose** — the question this area answers.
- **Inside** — the main information/views available.
- **Start here** — the first useful action.

Nested sub-tabs use a smaller **IN THIS VIEW** orientation strip. This avoids adding separate Overview tabs and keeps users close to the actual evidence.
