# MFOX v1.4.14 — registry studies, community ecosystem and company utilization

## Explore
- Adds **Registered studies** as a separate Explore view so public study-registry records are discoverable without conflating planned/ongoing studies with publication-derived assay evidence.
- Seeds six menstrual-fluid-relevant ClinicalTrials.gov records in `data/registry_studies.csv`.
- Registry records follow the main Explore Context / Technology / Sample filters when those fields are available.
- `Public dataset only` retains registry records only when a verified linked MFOX study has an indexed public dataset.

## Companies
- Removes the redundant public **Evidence source** link from company-directory cards when the company website already serves as the destination. The underlying source URL remains curated in the database.
- Rebuilds the utilization space as a connected **Material → Company → Utilization** Sankey plus a material-by-utilization co-occurrence heatmap and utilization summary cards.
- Keeps Company directory as the first view.

## Community
- Reorders tabs: **Collaboration groups → Researchers & labs → Companies & innovators → Geography → Join the Community**.
- Collaboration groups is now the opening view and includes community summary metrics before users dive into detailed directories.
- Clarifies the product split: Community → Companies is the **WHO / WHERE** organization view; top-level Companies is the **WHAT / HOW** utilization/evidence view.
- Geography now maps research institutions and companies together; companies use a distinct teal marker and map legend.

## Voting
- Candidate vote clicks prevent default/propagation so they do not trigger surrounding navigation behavior.
- New & Unreviewed outputs no longer fade while recalculating after a vote.
- Votes update the current session immediately. Disk persistence is attempted separately, so read-only deployments no longer make voting appear broken; a warning is shown if persistence is unavailable.
