# MFOX v1.4.15 — multi-registry discovery + stable voting

## Explore order
`Connections` is the first Explore view, followed by Studies & assays, Registered studies, Papers, Datasets, Projects/programs, and Labs & companies.

## Registry discovery
The earlier v1.4.14 registry table was a targeted seed and was not exhaustive. v1.4.15 broadens the curated discovery method:
- query `menstrual effluent`, `menstrual blood`, `menstrual fluid`, and `menstruum`;
- inspect registry identifiers found in publications and protocols;
- manually verify that menstrual-derived material is actually collected, analyzed, or used as the study object;
- retain registry records separately from publication-derived assay evidence.

Current curated registry sources:
- ClinicalTrials.gov
- ISRCTN
- German Clinical Trials Register (DRKS)
- Onderzoek met mensen / ToetsingOnline (Netherlands)

Notable additions include PEARL (NCT06332560), MultiMENDo (NCT06245512), PROTEO-ENDO (NCT06214260), the Diamens diagnostic validation study (NCT07553676), MEN-ENDO (NCT07578480), inVitD (NCT05050916), REPAIR (NCT07557992), ISRCTN72218532, and DRKS00036558.

## Voting
The custom browser-level delegated voting listener was removed. Voting now uses native Shiny `actionButton()` inputs with fixed DOM nodes. Each candidate card remains static after first render; only a small candidate-specific vote status output is reactive. This prevents the entire candidate list from being invalidated/re-rendered when a vote is clicked.
