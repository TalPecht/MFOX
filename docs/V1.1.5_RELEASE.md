# MFOX v1.1.5 — Contribution + ontology consolidation

- Added top-level **Contribute** portal for publication/manuscript, dataset, company/technology, researcher/lab, corrections and other menstrual-fluid resources.
- Submission form explicitly captures the terminology used for the sample and asks where/how menstrual fluid was used, designed to recover papers whose title/abstract do not expose menstrual-fluid sampling.
- Submissions enter `data/contributions.csv` with `Submitted` status and do not alter curated evidence automatically.
- Added curator-state model: Submitted → review → Accepted / Needs information / Out of scope.
- Home now links directly to Contribute.
- Strengthened the Home Explore evidence button with direct navbar navigation plus the existing Shiny observer.
- Consolidated redundant biospecimen classes in canonical CSV data: whole menstrual fluid, menstrual-fluid cells, tissue fragments, cells/tissue not further separated, cell-free menstrual fluid, EVs, cultured menstrual-derived cells, experimental derivatives.
- Retains v1.1.4 simplified Companies overview and prior planner/timeline/assay-transparency improvements.

Deployment note: writing a CSV from a deployed Shiny instance may not be persistent on all hosts. For a public deployment, connect the contribution form to a persistent store (e.g. database, Google Sheet/API, GitHub issue workflow, or form backend) while retaining curator review.
