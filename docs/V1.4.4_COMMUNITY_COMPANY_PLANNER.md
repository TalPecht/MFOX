- Company discovery here is organization-focused: location, utilization, technology/application, development stage, and evidence status.

## Plan a Study
- Replaced literal-only company matching with transparent overlap matching.
- A company can be surfaced when it overlaps the planned clinical context/disease, technology family, menstrual-fluid sample/material, or longitudinal-monitoring use.
- Each company card states the matched dimensions and retains development/evidence status.
- Suggestions indicate translational overlap only; they are not endorsements or evidence-quality rankings.
