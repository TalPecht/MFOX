# MFOX v1.4.11 — Functional + concise navigation

## Candidate voting
Voting in **New & Unreviewed** now uses native delegated JavaScript and sends one structured `candidate_vote` event to Shiny. The server saves the vote to `data/candidate_votes.csv` and shows an explicit success or write-error notification. MFOX Den shows the resulting community triage summary; voting remains triage only and never determines inclusion.

## Companies → Evidence & maturity
The tab now starts with a compact overview explaining the distinction between:
- **Development stage** — where a product/platform/service sits in development.
- **Evidence status** — the strongest public evidence type curated by MFOX.

A summary shows represented categories, followed by robust company cards. Missing values fall back to explicit “not yet categorized” text instead of breaking rendering.

## Landscape timeline
The previous timeline stacked category memberships. This could visually over-count a multi-assay study because one study could appear in several technology/sample categories. v1.4.11 changes the encoding:
- **Background bar:** unique studies (or unique assays) in that year.
- **Colored lines:** number of those studies/assays represented in each selected category.
- Category lines are **non-exclusive** and may overlap.

Example: a single study with transcriptomics and flow cytometry contributes **1** to the yearly study total, and **1** to each of those two technology lines.

## Explore cleanup
Explore is now the concise record-level knowledgebase view. It no longer repeats the large Explore-vs-Planner comparison cards. The relationship-count strip lives inside **Studies & assays**, and each Explore sub-tab starts with a short orientation strip.

Landscape and Explore now have separate jobs:
- **Landscape:** high-level patterns, concentration, gaps, and change over time.
- **Explore:** the individual studies, assays, papers, datasets, projects, labs and companies behind those patterns.
