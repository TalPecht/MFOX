# MFOX data license

Unless otherwise stated, the original MFOX-curated metadata contained in `data/` is intended for release under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0), subject to confirmation of the final copyright holder and institutional policy before formal public release.

CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## Important distinction

MFOX does **not** relicense third-party publications or the underlying molecular datasets to which it links. Publications, repository records, raw sequencing/proteomics/metabolomics files, and other external resources remain subject to the terms of their original publishers, repositories, and data providers.

The MFOX license applies to the original selection, organization, harmonization, annotations, and metadata authored for the MFOX knowledgebase to the extent those elements are copyrightable/licensable.

Before the first formal public release, replace this provisional wording with institution-approved copyright attribution if required.
